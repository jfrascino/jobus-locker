# Jobu's Locker

A candlelit 3D recreation of Pedro Cerrano's Jobu shrine from the Cleveland clubhouse in *Major League* (1989), built in three.js.

**Open it:** https://jfrascino.github.io/jobus-locker/

- Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap anything on the shrine to see what it is.
- The chips along the bottom jump to the locker, the shrine, Jobu, the offerings, the lower altar, the top shelf, the offering table and the film's own camera angle.
- Rituals: pour Jobu's rum, put out (or relight) his cigar, snuff the candles, and steal the rum (don't).
- Music: tap Music, or tap the Heart or RATT CD at Jobu's feet and choose "Put it on". The songs stream quietly from the bands' official YouTube uploads (Heart's Greatest Hits, RATT's Out of the Cellar).

The layout, props and colours were matched to frames from the film. Additions that aren't in the movie: the SULLY nameplate (the film's reads CERRANO), the Bacardi 151 bottle, the Heart and RATT CDs at Jobu's feet, Sully's cap on the top shelf, two snapshots pinned by the flag, and a shirt hanging in the next stall over.
