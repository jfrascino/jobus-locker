# Jobu's Locker

A candlelit 3D recreation of Pedro Cerrano's Jobu shrine from the Cleveland clubhouse in *Major League* (1989), built in three.js.

**Open it:** https://jfrascino.github.io/jobus-locker/

- Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap anything on the shrine to see what it is.
- The chips along the bottom jump to the locker, the shrine, Jobu, the offerings, the lower altar, the top shelf, the offering table and the film's own camera angle.
- Rituals: pour Jobu's rum, put out (or relight) his cigar, snuff the candles, and steal the rum (don't).
- Music: tap Music, or tap the Heart or RATT CD at Jobu's feet and choose "Put it on". The songs stream quietly from the bands' official YouTube uploads (Heart's Greatest Hits, RATT's Out of the Cellar). YouTube needs its player on screen while a song plays, so the − button folds it into a small cover button and pauses; tap that to pick up where it left off.

The layout, props and colours were matched to frames from the film. Additions that aren't in the movie: the SULLY nameplate (the film's reads CERRANO), the Bacardi 151 bottle, the Heart and RATT CDs and two sticks of Land O'Lakes ("Kurt's Bedroom Butter") at Jobu's feet, three bags of PURE ICE ("Kurt's Pleasure Cubes") leaning against the foot of the stall, Sully's cap on the top shelf, four snapshots pinned by the flag (the gourd hangs a little lower than in the film to keep them clear), and a shirt hanging in the next stall over.
